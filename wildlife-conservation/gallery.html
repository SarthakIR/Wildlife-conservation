Responsible encounters

Observe with respect.

Wildlife photography should never come at the cost of animal welfare or habitat health.

↔

Give space

Keep a safe distance. Never surround, chase, touch, or corner wild animals for a photograph.

◌

Leave no trace

Take litter home, stay on permitted paths, and never remove plants, eggs, shells, or other natural items.

♡

Put welfare first

Avoid baiting, flash where it may disturb animals, and wildlife attractions that force contact.
